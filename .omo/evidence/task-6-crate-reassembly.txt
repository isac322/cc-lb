T6 crate reassembly evidence
============================

Scope
-----
Created unpublished `cc-lb-request-log` as the stable persisted request-log
read-model. Its only cc-lb dependency is `cc-lb-domain`; its external
dependencies are `serde`, `serde_json`, and `uuid`. It has no Tokio dependency
and defines no `Persisted*` mirror type.

Moved owned items
-----------------
* `RequestEvent`, with `routing_trace: Option<cc_lb_domain::RoutingTrace>` and
  `internal_errors: Vec<cc_lb_domain::InternalError>` directly.
* `RequestEventPartial`, `RequestEventUpdate`, `FinalRequestEventUpdate`, and
  `RequestEventPhase`.
* `RequestEventUpstream`, `RequestCacheState`, `RequestCacheBreakpoint`,
  `RequestCacheLookbackPrefix`, and `RequestCacheBreakpointSource`.
* `HeaderSnapshot` and `CostBreakdown`.

Ownership and bridges
---------------------
`cc-lb-contract` retains Tokio transport only: `RequestEventBus`,
`BusReceiver`, `LifecycleBusReceiver`, and
`DEFAULT_LIFECYCLE_BROADCAST_CAPACITY`. It has a narrow `#[doc(hidden)]`
re-export bridge for every T6-owned DTO. `event_bus.rs` imports the update
payload directly from request-log, so no DTO definition remains in contract.

Direct engine, pricing, control, admin, and server consumers now import moved
DTOs from `cc_lb_request_log`. `cc-lb-storage-api` deliberately retains its
existing re-export through the contract bridge until its T9 ownership move; the
SQLite T0 fixture therefore exercises `storage-api -> contract bridge ->
request-log` exactly as required.

RED-first and serde artifact
----------------------------
The focused request-log fixture test was created before the DTO definition.
`cargo test -p cc-lb-request-log --test reassembly_serde_fixtures` first exited
101 with E0432 (`cc_lb_request_log::RequestEvent` missing). After the move, the
test deserializes `tests/fixtures/reassembly/request_event.json`, asserts its
populated domain `routing_trace` and one internal error, and compares
`serde_json::to_string(&event)` byte-for-byte with the fixture. It passed in
both required deterministic runs.

* empty scans for `PersistedRoutingTrace|PersistedInternalError|Persisted` and

LSP and review
--------------
LSP diagnostics were clean for request-log and contract. The daemon timed out
twice for engine, pricing, admin, control, and server, so the required
successful targeted builds and workspace `--all-targets` check provide compiler
coverage for those re-points. All new request-log Rust files are below 250 pure
LOC under the repository's measurement. Modules own cache values, header/cost
values, persisted event schema, and live update DTOs separately. No unsafe,
production unwrap/expect, untyped boundary, new enum wildcard, or logging was
introduced.

Cleanup and risks
-----------------
No process, credential, network resource, migration, or temporary database was
created. The only intentional compatibility surface is the doc-hidden contract
bridge, scheduled for removal with the remaining contract decomposition in T11;
storage-api's bridge-based re-export is scheduled for its T9 ownership move.
