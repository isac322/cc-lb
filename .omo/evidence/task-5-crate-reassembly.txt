T5 crate reassembly evidence
============================

Scope
-----
Created unpublished `cc-lb-quota` with direct cc-lb dependencies exactly
`cc-lb-domain` and `cc-lb-storage-api`. It owns rate-limit/unified-quota
header parsing, quota-sample construction, and plan-capacity classification.
No quota value types were redefined: `RateLimitKind`, `RateLimitObservation`,
`SubscriptionQuotaDataState`, `SubscriptionQuotaCandidateSnapshot`,
`SubscriptionTier`, and `PlanInfo` remain in `cc-lb-domain`.

Moved owned items
-----------------
* `rate_limit_headers`: `UnifiedQuotaObservation`,
  `parse_anthropic_rate_limit_headers`, `parse_anthropic_unified_headers`,
  `clamp_utilization_fraction`, and `percent_to_utilization_fraction`.
* `plan_capacity`: `PRO_CAPACITY_RATIO`, `TierKey`, `ParseTierKeyError`,
  `PlanTierClassification`, `classify_plan_tier`, `plan_capacity_ratio`, and
  `tier_key_from_seed_ratio`. `PlanInfo` is consumed/re-exported from domain.
* quota samples: `build_subscription_quota_samples` and
  `unified_observation_to_sample`.

`cc-lb-engine/src/rate_limit_headers.rs`, `plan_capacity.rs`, `lifecycle.rs`,
`subscription_quota_events.rs`, `lifecycle_subscription_quota_subscriber.rs`,
`cc-lb-storage-api/src/upstream_subscription_quota.rs`, and every caller.

`subscription_quota_events::unified_observation_to_sample` re-export. This
intentionally preserves the existing cc-lb-admin and cc-lb-server imports
until T15 decouples them from engine.
